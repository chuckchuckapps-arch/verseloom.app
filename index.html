Features

Everything you need to memorize Scripture deeply.

🧠

Spaced Repetition (SM-2)

The same algorithm behind Anki schedules your reviews at the exact moment you're about to forget — maximizing retention with minimal time.

📖

Progressive Reveal

Start with the full verse, then half the words disappear, then only first letters remain, until you can recall it completely from memory.

📚

Preset Tracks

Begin immediately with Psalm 23 and 10 of the most beloved verses — or type in any passage you want to memorize.

🔔

Daily Reminders

A gentle morning notification tells you how many verses are ready for review — no streak guilt, just a quiet invitation.

✈️

Fully Offline

Everything lives on your device. No internet required, no account, no backend. Open the app on a plane and it works perfectly.

🆓

Completely Free

How it works

From first reading to lifelong memory.

1

Add a verse

Type in any reference and text, or import from a preset track like Psalm 23. KJV and WEB are public-domain translations you can paste freely.

2

Work through the four passes

Read the full verse → fill in the blanks → reconstruct from first letters → recall completely from memory with optional word hints.

3

Rate your recall

After each session, choose Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. The SM-2 algorithm uses your rating to schedule the perfect next review date.

4

Watch it move to Mastered

Review intervals grow from 1 day → 6 days → weeks → months. Once a verse reaches the Mastered section, it's truly in your long-term memory.

🔒

Your data stays on your device

VerseLoom collects zero personal data. Your verses and review history never leave your phone — no analytics, no tracking, no account. Read the full privacy policy →
